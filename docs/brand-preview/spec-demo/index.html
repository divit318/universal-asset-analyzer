What a sample can’t show and what to imagine instead

Everything you can click here is real interaction on canned data. These are the parts of the spec that only exist in the full build — listed so you can judge them on the description rather than assume they were forgotten.

The command line doesn’t parse. In the product it takes tickers, filter sentences, and commands like why NVDA 81 or wl ASML buy under 900, answering in document flow below the line. Here it’s display only.

The margin only answers for marked elements. Hover the price, the conviction number, the TOC, “no basis,” and a few others (they brighten). In the product, every figure on every page answers — source, age, method — after a 300ms hold.

Numbers settle from live data. The tick-through-intermediates arrival happens here only where scripted (price probe, trim sheet, run). In the product it fires on every real market update, which is what makes a settling number read as news.

Compare’s physical columns aren’t here. Dragging a ticker column to reorder, tearing it away to remove, typing into the empty header stub — pointer choreography worth building once, not twice. Judge it from the spec’s description.

Reading detection is approximated. The IC report’s TOC fills after ~1.5 seconds of a section being in view. The product’s threshold is reading-speed honest — a fast scroll past a section does not count as reading it.

The run is compressed ~40×. Real runs take minutes and survive a closed laptop lid, resuming with PAUSED AT 07:12 — resumed in the log. The self-correction you’ll see mid-stream is scripted here; in the product it happens when the grounding pass catches the model, roughly once per run.

No sounds. The pen-cap tick on recording a decision and the low end-of-run tone are described in the spec; both ship off by default anyway.

Scale isn’t demonstrated. The screener re-rank must stay smooth at 400+ rows; this sample proves the grammar at 9. Performance is an engineering promise, not a design one.

Mobile gestures. Swipe-to-defer with the date stamping on is spec-only; a static mobile frame is in the earlier /terminal/ sample.

Nothing persists. Refresh resets the docket, the trims, and the retired note. The product’s whole point is that it never forgets; the sample’s whole point is that it always does.
