BRAND SPECIMEN · v1.0 · PHASE 0 — NOTHING IMPLEMENTED
◆ THE IDENTITY

The Instrument of Record. Evidence in ink. Verdicts in brass.

UAA is a private financial institution with a staff of one. The brand renders the two things such an institution deals in — evidence and judgment — as two visibly different materials. This page is the identity made visible: colors, voices, components, and a page before & after.

Prepared for the committee of one
01

Color — earned by judgment

The interface is achromatic ink on graphite. Hue exists only where meaning exists: brass for verdicts and touchpoints, green/red/orange for the market. Brass is rationed to particle scale — if a screen ever looks “gold,” the system is being misused.

Pending = outline square. Resolved = filled brass diamond. The loading mark's story, told at component scale — steppers, timelines, graph nodes, checked states.

Provenance chips — the record is sacred

Square chip = raw data. Diamond chip = derived judgment. Always timestamped. Provenance as ornament.

List grammar — the accepted call is brass

  • Unit economics improve with each cohort; churn is structural, not cyclical
  • Enter a half position; add on a close above the 200-day
  • Management guidance history: 7 of 8 quarters delivered
Report bullets are ink diamonds; the accepted recommendation's bullet is brass.

Empty state — a standing order

The book is empty. Add your first position to bring the desk online.

Never a mood, never an apology. Outline square + one instruction + one action. The square resolves to a diamond when real data arrives.

Voice — the senior analyst who respects your time

ContextNeverAlways
VERDICT "Our AI thinks this could be a great opportunity!" Trim. Concentration in semis is 2.4× your policy cap. — verdict first, evidence after.
ERROR "Oops! Something went wrong 😕" Ollama is offline. Start it to resume AI analysis.
EMPTY "No holdings yet" The book is empty. Add your first position to bring the desk online.
LONG TASK "Hang tight, this may take a while…" Running the committee — typically 3–15 minutes on a local model.
MARKETING "Blazing-fast, AI-powered, seamless insights" 9 agents, run in parallel, on your hardware. Nothing leaves your machine.
